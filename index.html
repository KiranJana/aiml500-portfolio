About

Professional bio

I'm a backend software engineer. My day-to-day work is building and supporting production services and data pipelines, which has taught me that the hardest problems often hide in data that looks fine until someone reads the report. When I started, I couldn't fix a production failure on my own; I learned by working through those failures one at a time.

I'm now studying AI and machine learning in the AIML-500 course at Indiana Wesleyan University. I use AI tools at work, mostly for debugging, and I've learned that their answers still need checking before anything ships. My goal is to help my team adopt AI tools in a way we can trust.

Value proposition

I build small AI tools for engineering teams and test them the way I test my code. I try to break each rule on purpose and write down what I find, so other teams can reuse it.
